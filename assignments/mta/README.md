# MTA Ridership Analysis
In this assignment, you are given two CSV files. One containing the daily ridership count of various New York City public transportation lines, starting in 2020, and going up until January of 2025, and the other containing the estimated population of New York City for each year during the same period. 

Your task is to generate a new CSV file that contains the ratio of the ridership count to the population for each year.


The columns of the mta_ridership.csv file are as follows:
- `Date`: The date of the ridership count in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
- `Subways: Total Estimated Ridership`: The total estimated ridership for subways on that date.
- `Buses: Total Estimated Ridership`: The total estimated ridership for buses on that date.
- `LIRR: Total Estimated Ridership`: The total estimated ridership for the Long Island Rail Road on that date.
- `Metro-North: Total Estimated Ridership`: The total estimated ridership for the Metro-North Railroad on that date.
- `Access-A-Ride: Total Scheduled Trips`: The total scheduled trips for Access-A-Ride on that date.
- `Bridges and Tunnels: Total Traffic`: The total traffic for bridges and tunnels on that date.
- `Staten Island Railway: Total Estimated Ridership`: The total estimated ridership for the Staten Island Railway on that date.

The columns of the population.csv file are as follows:
- `Year`: The year of the population estimate.
- `Population`: The estimated population of New York City for that year.
