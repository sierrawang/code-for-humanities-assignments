[156] In the Carcassonne MS this chapter is not separated from the previous one. In the BnF MS, the text of cap. III.3 was inserted after the first seven words of III.2, which then continues as a separate chapter after III.3. The text of the Carcassonne MS is followed here. But since III.3 was added to the margin of the Carcassonne MS (at a later date), the error in the BnF MS probably results from a scribal error in determining where to insert the added text.
